What the checks are

  • Arithmetic: subtotal plus tax must equal total, tolerance two cents.
  • Duplicates: invoice number reused across documents.
  • Completeness: issue date, due date, vendor, number and total all present.
  • Sanity: due date cannot precede issue date, amounts above a threshold are held.
  • Readability: documents without a text layer are sent to OCR, never parsed on a guess.
